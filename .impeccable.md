## Design Context

### Users
Players interested in freestyle rap, hip-hop culture, career simulators, and mobile-friendly progression games. They need to understand their MC's current status quickly, choose one next action, and feel the rise from bedroom/underground to larger stages.

### Brand Personality
Underground, focused, aspirational. The interface should feel like a playable career notebook layered over a pixel-art world, not like a generic analytics dashboard.

### Aesthetic Direction
Pixel-art hip-hop simulator with restrained faux-3D environments. The scene should carry atmosphere; UI should be clear, compact, and organized. Dark, club-like backgrounds are acceptable, but panels must remain readable and purposeful.

### Design Principles
- One clear primary decision at a time: the next action or battle choice should be obvious.
- Use hierarchy before decoration: reduce competing panels and keep repeated stats grouped.
- Preserve the world: keep scenery visible, but never let it obscure important text.
- App-ready clarity: large touch targets, stable panel zones, no overlapping labels.
- Progress should feel tangible: stage, level, resources, and next goal must be readable at a glance.
